Call prep · for you, not for MACK1 / 6 · Cloud in one page

How the cloud works. Three ideas, nothing more.

Almost every question MACK asks will come back to one of these three ideas. If you understand them, you can explain any option.

1

Storage

A company cupboard that lives on the internet.

All project files sit in one place owned by MACK (Google, Microsoft or Dropbox). Nobody keeps "their" copy. If a laptop dies, nothing is lost.

2

Sync

The cupboard shows up as a drive on every laptop.

People open and save files as usual, from a drive letter such as G:. Changes travel to everyone automatically, including deletions and mistakes.

3

Backup

A photocopy kept in another building, by another company.

A separate service quietly copies the cupboard several times a day and keeps older copies. It is only used when something goes wrong.

The one thing to remember

Cloud storage is not a backup.

Because sync copies everything to everyone, a deleted folder, a drawing saved over, or a virus also spreads everywhere instantly. Google keeps old versions for only 30 days. A backup is a separate copy with a separate password, so it still has yesterday's healthy files.

Words you may hear, in plain English
Version historyGoogle remembers earlier saves of a file for 30 days, so you can go back to an older version.
Bin / TrashDeleted files wait here for 30 days before they are gone for good.
ArchiveFinished projects moved to a read-only folder so nobody changes them by accident.
NAS / serverA box of hard disks inside the studio. Fast on the office network, but MACK has to buy and look after it.
Offline driveA portable hard disk kept unplugged in a safe. No internet or password breach can reach it.
TB (terabyte)1 TB is about 1,000 GB, roughly 200,000 phone photos. MACK expects 2–3 TB to start.

Call prep2 / 6 · A normal day

What it looks like day to day. What happens when things go wrong.

Use these as examples on the call. Clients understand stories better than technology.

Morning: open a drawing

The designer opens G:\MACK Projects\Garden House\03 CAD and works in AutoCAD as usual. On save, it uploads by itself.

Sharing with a client

Instead of email attachments or WhatsApp, send a link to the Final Deliverables folder, for that person only, expiring in 30 days.

"I deleted a folder"

Restore it from the Bin in a minute. Under 30 days: Bin. After that: the backup.

"I saved over the final plan"

Right-click → version history → pick yesterday's version. Under 30 days: Google. Older: the backup.

Laptop stolen or dead

Nothing was only on it. Lock its access from the admin page and sign in on a new laptop. Back to work in about an hour.

Internet goes down

Folders marked "available offline" keep working. Changes upload when the internet returns. A phone hotspot works for urgent sends.

Virus / ransomware

Google's sync app notices and pauses. Roll files back to the clean version, or restore from the backup taken before the attack.

Studio fire or break-in

Main copy and backup are both online. Finished projects are also on a drive kept off-site. Nothing important lives only in the studio.

Call prep6 / 6 · Their likely questions

Questions they may ask. Short, honest answers.

"Is our data safe with Google?"

It's encrypted and protected by two-step sign-in for every person. The bigger risk is our own mistakes, which is what the backup is for.

"Why pay for backup if Google already stores it?"

Google copies mistakes too, and keeps old versions only 30 days. The backup is a separate copy at a separate company.

"Why not just use external hard disks?"

People forget to copy, and disks fail or get stolen. We use disks only as an extra copy of finished projects.

"Why not buy a server like other studios?"

At our size it costs about ₹2.6 lakh up front plus upkeep, for no gain. We'll add one if the team grows or Revit needs it.

"What if the internet goes down?"

Active projects are kept offline on laptops, so work continues and uploads later. A phone hotspot covers urgent sends.

"What if someone leaves?"

Files belong to MACK, not the person. We switch off their access the same day; nothing walks out.

"Can we stop or switch later?"

Yes. The files are normal DWG, RVT and PDF files and can be downloaded any time. The backup and drives hold copies too.

"Who owns the accounts?"

MACK does. The accounts are registered and paid by MACK; we only have admin access, which they can remove.

Before the call
  • Have this pack open on page 3
  • Note their answers to the 8 questions
  • Don't promise a price in dollars; say "about"
  • Confirm Google's price on screen from India
  • Ask who will be MACK's admin contact
  • End with a choice: A, B or C
If you only say one thing

"All your projects in one company account everyone can work from, a separate backup copying it three times a day, and finished work also kept on drives you hold, for about ₹7,300 a month."
