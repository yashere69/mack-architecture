# 10 — Paise ki baat: conversation script (PRIVATE — deck mein nahi)

Use this **after** the deck, once Ayushi agrees with the scope. Address her the way you normally do (Ayushi / ma'am / Ayushi ji). The script avoids gendered verb forms ("karunga/karungi") so it works for anyone. Fill in your own numbers before the meeting (see file 09).

---

### 1) Pehle scope confirm karo

> "Toh yeh poora plan tha. Aapko kaisa laga — direction sahi lag rahi hai? Kuch add ya remove karna ho toh abhi bata dijiye, taaki scope bilkul clear rahe."

*(Unhe bolne do. Jo bhi change bolein, note karo.)*

### 2) Kaam ke do hisse samjhao

> "Is kaam ke basically do hisse hain. Pehla hai setup: domain, studio email, website launch, Google Business Profile, analytics aur project pages. Yeh pehle do-teen mahine ka kaam hai, aur thoda heavy hai.
> Doosra hai ongoing kaam: har mahine monitoring, report, naye projects publish karna, profile maintain karna. Yeh chhota hai, par continuous hai."

### 3) Structure propose karo

> "Isliye mujhe lagta hai sabse fair structure yeh rahega: setup ke liye ek fixed amount, jo milestones se juda ho, teen hisson mein. Har payment tab, jab aapko kuch concrete dikhe: kickoff pe, website launch pe, aur Google profile aur project pages complete hone pe.
> Aur launch ke baad ek monthly amount, ongoing kaam ke liye."

### 4) Third-party costs clear karo

> "Domain, Google Workspace, hosting, in sab ka kharcha seedha studio ke account se jaayega, mere through nahi. Sab kuch MACK ke naam pe rahega, aur poora transparent rahega."

### 5) Number bolo, phir ruk jao

> "Setup ke liye mera proposal **₹______** hai, teen hisson mein. Aur launch ke baad **₹______ monthly**, pehle teen mahine ke liye. Uske baad month-to-month, ek mahine ke notice ke saath."

*(Number bolne ke baad chup raho. Explain mat karo jab tak woh kuch na poochein.)*

---

## Agar woh yeh kahein…

**"Thoda zyada lag raha hai."**
> "Samajh sakte hain. Ek option yeh hai ki scope thoda chhota kar dein. Pehle sirf foundation, website launch aur Google profile, aur portfolio aur publications wala kaam baad mein. Usse setup amount kam ho jaayega. Kaam ki quality wahi rahegi, bas scope chhota hoga."

**"Hum stipend jaisa kuch soch rahe the."**
> "Thank you, yeh sunke accha laga. Bas ek baat: is kaam mein accounts, website launch, Google profile aur monthly reporting ki zimmedari hai. Isliye mera suggestion hai ki ise milestones aur monthly deliverables ke saath tie karein. Aapko har stage pe pata rahega ki kya mila, aur mujhe bhi clarity rahegi."

**"Ek hi monthly amount rakh lete hain."**
> "Haan, yeh bhi ho sakta hai. Tab ise 12 mahine ka engagement rakh lete hain, ek flat monthly amount ke saath, jismein setup bhi spread ho jaaye. Scope aur milestones wahi rahenge."

**"Results ki guarantee hai?"**
> "Kaam, timeline aur honest reporting, yeh meri guarantee hai. Ranking ki guarantee koi bhi honestly nahi de sakta. Google khud kehta hai ki better ranking ke liye pay nahi kiya ja sakta. Par progress har mahine measure hogi, aur aapko report mein dikhegi."

**"Agency se kyun nahi?"**
> "Agencies ka structure alag hota hai, unke overheads hote hain. Yahan aap seedha mere saath kaam karenge, aur poori zimmedari meri hogi, setup se le kar monthly reports tak."

**"Pehle sirf website aur email kar lo."**
> "Bilkul. Toh pehle Milestone 1 aur 2 kar lete hain. Bas ek suggestion: Google Maps wala listing ka issue jaldi solve karna accha rahega, kyunki koi bhi studio ka naam search kare toh wahi dikhta hai."

---

### 6) Close (bina pressure ke)

> "Aap aaram se soch lijiye. Kal tak aapko ek short written summary mil jaayegi: scope, milestones, payment structure, sab ek jagah. Agar sab theek lage, toh ek 90-minute working session rakh lete hain aur shuru karte hain."

## Kya NAHI bolna

- "Ma'am, mera package ₹X ka hai." (Package language sounds like a shop.)
- "Jo aapko theek lage, de dena." (Sounds unsure, and you'll be underpaid.)
- "Hum log / meri team…" if it's just you. (Don't pretend to be an agency.)
- "#1 pe la denge", "leads guaranteed", "traffic double ho jaayega". (Bluff. It breaks trust later.)
- Number bolne ke baad: "…matlab thoda kam bhi ho sakta hai." (Never negotiate against yourself. Reduce scope, not price.)
- Lambi technical explanation (DNS, DKIM…). Keep the money talk about **scope → structure → number**.
