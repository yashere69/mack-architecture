S-02

Decide first: the three blockers

Nothing else can start properly until these are settled. Ask; don't assume.

Blocker 1

Studio identity & Maps listing

"What is the exact studio name on signage? How should the existing Google listing at the studio address represent MACK?"

Google allows one profile per business per location.

Blocker 2

Domain

"Do you own a domain? Which one should we use?"

Email, Search Console and the website all hang off it. Register it in MACK's name.

Blocker 3

Account owner

"Who at the studio should own the accounts? I'll be added with access."

Nothing gets registered in your name.

Also confirm (not blockers, but needed soon)
  • Services MACK wants to be found for (architecture, interiors, furniture…)
  • New studios: where, when, staffed, with signage?
  • Project facts: place, type, year, status, brief, photographer (about 10 min per project via a form)
  • Team & files: who needs email, where files live now, which design software
  • Quietly, later: the principal's Council of Architecture registration before using "Architect" as the Google category

What to do, and what not to

Essential

  • Domain + launch with indexing on
  • Search Console + sitemap
  • One verified Maps profile
  • Same name/address/phone everywhere
  • Real project facts and stories
  • Analytics with enquiry tracking
  • Faster home page on mobile

Useful

  • Service pages
  • Image credits
  • Links between related projects
  • Archello / Architizer
  • Bing Places
  • Dashboard
  • Review requests

Optional

  • Journal / blog
  • Apple Business listing
  • Paid SEO tool for one month
  • Profile posts (real news only)

Don't

  • City pages without a studio
  • Buying links, directory blasts
  • Keywords in the profile name
  • Fake or rewarded reviews
  • AI-written bulk blogs
  • FAQ schema, llms.txt

S-06

After launch: the monthly routine

About 6–10 hours a month.

Every month
  • Search Console: queries, indexing, errors
  • Maps profile: reply to reviews, check edits, add real photos
  • Publish new projects (if any)
  • Test the contact form; check uptime, hosting usage, backup
  • Send the one-page report with next actions
Every quarter
  • Review meeting with the studio (45 min)
  • Refresh the keyword map
  • Access and permissions audit
  • Test-restore a file from backup
  • Update website software

S-10

Money Private

Never in the deck. Full version: private/09 and private/10.

Recommended structure (hybrid)
  1. Setup fee for M1–M4, paid in 3 parts: 40% at kickoff · 30% at website launch · 30% when the profile and project pages are done
  2. Monthly fee after launch: 3-month minimum, then 30 days' notice
  3. Third-party costs (domain, Workspace, hosting, NAS) paid by MACK directly, no markup
  4. Extras (e.g. a new studio location) priced per item
Why not the others
  • Stipend: sounds like an intern, no clear scope
  • Monthly only: the heavy setup months get underpaid
  • Milestones only: monthly work never ends
  • Plan B: if she wants one number, a flat monthly fee for 12 months with setup spread across it
Work out your numbers (your rate; no invented market rates)
Setup ≈ 50 hours × your hourly rate + 10–15% buffer
Monthly ≈ 8 hours × your hourly rate

Sanity-check by asking 2–3 Mumbai freelancers or small agencies what they'd quote for similar work. If the price comes down, reduce the scope, not your rate.

S-12

Never do

These break trust or Google's rules.

  • Promise rankings, traffic, leads or footfall
  • Own MACK's accounts yourself
  • Create a second Maps profile at an address that already has one
  • Put opening hours on an appointment-only studio's profile
  • Buy links, or write or reward reviews
  • Present renders as built work
  • Treat Google Drive as the backup
